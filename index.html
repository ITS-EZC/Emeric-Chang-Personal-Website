About

I'm a student at Columbia University pursuing dual degrees in Political Science and Computer Science — because the most interesting problems live where those two fields collide.

My work spans state legislative offices, AI policy research, federal prosecution, and the archery range. Whether I'm drafting a constituent newsletter, coordinating a network of student AI ambassadors, or competing at national tournaments, I'm drawn to the same question: how do systems work, and how do they fail the people inside them?

I write for the Sundial, research for the Columbia Policy Institute, and believe — perhaps stubbornly — that a 4.00 GPA and a bow and arrow are not mutually exclusive.
