About

I'm an undergraduate at Columbia University studying Political Science and Computer Science, interested in the collision of law, AI, and society as a whole.

My work spans state legislative offices, tech-policy research, federal prosecution, and international AI youth activism organizations. Whether I'm drafting a constituent newsletter, coordinating a network of student AI ambassadors, or drafting a legal motion, I'm drawn to the same question: how do systems work, how do they fail the people inside them, and how can we fix them?

I live by three guiding mottos: 1. Stay Hungry, Stay Foolish; 2. Ite, Inflammate Omnia; 3. Memento Mori Ergo Carpe Diem
