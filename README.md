
Emeric's Personal Website — Design Plan
Aesthetic

Deep black background with neon electric blue and gold accents (the gold nods to legal/constitutional formality, the blue to cyberpunk/AI)
Serif fonts (think formal legal document) for section headers, mixed with monospace/terminal fonts for details and body — the tension between the two is the aesthetic
Subtle glitch effects, scanlines, and circuit/neural-net texture in the background
Sections styled like annotated case files or legal articles, but with a neon-lit, futuristic treatment

Sections

Hero — Your name large, a short punchy tagline, animated terminal-style text
About — Short bio blending your academic identity and interests
Academics — Columbia coursework, highlights (CS + Comp Lit), styled like a docket
Experience — Journalism, writing, projects — formatted like case briefs or exhibits
Interests & Hobbies — A more freeform section with a warmer feel

Interactions

Hover effects with neon glow
Section transitions with a subtle glitch/flicker
Smooth scroll navigation
